Retail Sales and Customer Analysis¶

Business question: What drives this store's revenue, and how important are repeat customers?

This notebook audits UCI Online Retail, defines positive purchase sales, loads SQLite, and analyzes monthly trends, products, countries, repeat customers, and cohort retention. Run cells from top to bottom using the project .venv kernel. Purchase revenue is in GBP and does not subtract returns; it is not net revenue. Findings and validation appear at the end.

Initial audit results and decisions¶

The source contains 541,909 invoice lines and eight columns, dated 1 December 2010 to 9 December 2011. Invoice lines are not orders.

Issue Affected rows Decision for the next analysis stage
Missing customer ID 135,080 Preserve for overall purchase sales; exclude from customer metrics
Missing description 1,454 Preserve; use StockCode for product grouping and inspect labels
Cancellation invoice 9,288 Preserve in raw data; exclude from purchase sales and review separately
Quantity at or below zero 10,624 Preserve in raw data; exclude from positive purchase sales; investigate returns and adjustments
Unit price at or below zero 2,517 Preserve in raw data; exclude from purchase sales; inspect free items and adjustments
Exact duplicate rows 5,268 Flag and retain pending evidence; identical lines may be legitimate, so do not automatically drop

Counts overlap. No rows have been removed. December 2011 is a partial month and should be labelled or excluded from full-month comparisons. A lack of transactions on individual days does not prove that a month is incomplete; December 2010 ends on 23 December and requires calendar context before interpreting it.

Next: build the positive purchase-sales dataset, load SQLite, and validate totals before drawing business conclusions. Purchase revenue will not subtract returns and cancellations, so it must not be labelled net revenue.

Create purchase sales and SQLite database¶

Keep positive quantities and prices, excluding cancellation invoices. Missing customer IDs remain in overall sales and are excluded from customer analysis. Duplicate lines remain pending review. Purchase revenue does not subtract returns and is not net revenue. Run the earlier cells first.

Top products¶

Which products generate the most purchase revenue and sell the most units? Group by StockCode and use the most frequent description for display. These rankings include all codes in the purchase-sales dataset, including possible postage or service codes; inspect the leaders before calling them merchandise bestsellers. Revenue does not measure profit. Retained duplicate lines and large wholesale purchases can affect rankings.

# Group by product code so changing descriptions do not split a product.
# Use each code's most frequent nonblank description as its display label.
with sqlite3.connect(processed / "retail.db") as conn:
    product_sales = pd.read_sql_query("""
WITH product_totals AS (
    SELECT StockCode,
           SUM(LineRevenue) AS purchase_revenue,
           SUM(Quantity) AS units,
           COUNT(DISTINCT InvoiceNo) AS orders
    FROM sales
    GROUP BY StockCode
), description_counts AS (
    SELECT StockCode, TRIM(Description) AS description, COUNT(*) AS occurrences
    FROM sales
    WHERE Description IS NOT NULL AND TRIM(Description) <> ''
    GROUP BY StockCode, TRIM(Description)
), ranked_descriptions AS (
    SELECT StockCode, description,
           ROW_NUMBER() OVER (
               PARTITION BY StockCode ORDER BY occurrences DESC, description
           ) AS rank
    FROM description_counts
)
SELECT p.StockCode, COALESCE(d.description, '(Description missing)') AS description,
       p.purchase_revenue, p.units, p.orders
FROM product_totals p
LEFT JOIN ranked_descriptions d ON p.StockCode = d.StockCode AND d.rank = 1
ORDER BY p.purchase_revenue DESC, p.StockCode
    """, conn)

top_revenue_products = product_sales.sort_values(

).head(10)
top_unit_products = product_sales.sort_values(

Repeat customers¶

A repeat customer has at least two distinct purchase invoices during the observed period. The denominator for customer shares is identified purchasing customers; the denominator for their revenue shares is purchase revenue with a customer ID. Missing IDs are not treated as one anonymous customer. This retrospective grouping is not a forecast: customers first observed later have less time to make another purchase.

In [16]:
with sqlite3.connect(processed / "retail.db") as conn:
    repeat_summary = pd.read_sql_query("""
WITH customer_summary AS (
    SELECT CustomerID, COUNT(DISTINCT InvoiceNo) AS orders,
           SUM(LineRevenue) AS purchase_revenue
    FROM sales
    WHERE CustomerID IS NOT NULL
    GROUP BY CustomerID
)
SELECT CASE WHEN orders >= 2 THEN 'Repeat' ELSE 'One purchase' END AS customer_type,
       COUNT(*) AS customers, SUM(orders) AS orders,
       SUM(purchase_revenue) AS purchase_revenue
FROM customer_summary
GROUP BY customer_type
ORDER BY customer_type
    """, conn)
repeat_summary["customer_share_pct"] = 100 * repeat_summary["customers"] / repeat_summary["customers"].sum()
repeat_summary["identified_revenue_share_pct"] = 100 * repeat_summary["purchase_revenue"] / repeat_summary["purchase_revenue"].sum()

Monthly cohort retention¶

Cohorts are based on the first observed purchase month, not necessarily the customer's first-ever purchase. Retention is the fraction of that original cohort making a purchase in a later calendar month; it does not require continuous monthly purchasing.

The main retention analysis ends in November 2011, excluding the partial December 2011 window. Observed months with no returning customers are zero; future, unobserved months are blank. Cohorts have different follow-up lengths. The first observed cohort can include longstanding customers and should not be interpreted as newly acquired customers.

In [18]:

cohort_cutoff = pd.Period("2011-11", freq="M")

Cancellation review and duplicate sensitivity¶

Cancellation lines are kept separate from the positive purchase-sales view. Their signed line value is not a fully matched refund measure: invoice cancellations, returns, adjustments, and purchase timing are not reconciled. Do not subtract this total from purchase sales and call the result validated net revenue.

Exact duplicate source rows are retained because an identical line can represent a legitimate repeated entry. The sensitivity calculation shows how much purchase revenue changes if those identical purchase lines are removed; it does not establish the correct treatment.
